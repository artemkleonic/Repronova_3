Jurisdictional Legal Guides

Choose a jurisdiction to open practical legal guides and risk overviews.

Mexico

Legal planning for surrogacy in Mexico begins with the particular state and the proposed programme structure. The location of treatment, the surrogate’s residence and the intended place of birth all need to be identified before contracts are signed.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Georgia

A Georgian reproductive programme needs to be assessed against the legal rules for the particular treatment and family situation. Access to donor treatment and access to surrogacy should be examined separately.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Ukraine

Ukraine has a defined legal framework for gestational surrogacy, alongside significant practical challenges arising from the war. Legal planning should address both the intended programme and the arrangements needed if treatment, pregnancy care or birth cannot proceed at the original location.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Armenia

Eligibility is a central issue when considering surrogacy in Armenia. The intended parents’ circumstances and the surrogate’s citizenship can affect whether a proposed arrangement falls within the permitted framework.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

United States

A US surrogacy programme should be assessed at state level, with separate attention to the intended parents’ home-country requirements. The relevant law may depend on where the surrogate lives, where treatment takes place and where the child is expected to be born.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Kyrgyzstan

A proposed surrogacy programme in Kyrgyzstan needs a current assessment of eligibility, medical requirements and the route to legal parentage. Foreign intended parents should obtain a written explanation of how the rules apply to them before signing or making substantial payments.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Colombia

Assessment of the legal basis for a proposed programme, parentage and birth registration, contractual responsibilities and recognition of the child’s status abroad.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Albania

Review of treatment and programme proposals, the applicable enacted rules, the route to parentage and the documents needed after birth.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Northern Cyprus

Legal assessment of arrangements involving treatment in Northern Cyprus, including programmes where pregnancy care or birth takes place in another jurisdiction.

READ LEGAL GUIDE

Ghana

Review of surrogacy proposals involving Ghana, assisted-reproduction birth registration, the proposed parentage procedure and cross-border documentation.

READ LEGAL GUIDE
