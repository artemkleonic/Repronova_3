About us

REPRONOVA Fertility Legal Solutions is an independent legal and strategic advisory platform in international fertility and reproductive law.

We support clients in complex cross-border matters involving surrogacy, gamete and embryo donation, deferred parenthood, posthumous reproduction, parentage, citizenship and ART-related documentation. Our work focuses on legal strategy, due diligence, contract review, second legal opinions and jurisdictional risk assessment.

REPRONOVA does not operate as a surrogacy or donor agency, does not recruit or match surrogates or donors, and does not accept hidden referral, success or commission fees from clinics, agencies or commercial program operators.
