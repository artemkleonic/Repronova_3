Ethics and Independence

Version dated 13 September 2026. Effective upon publication.

Our role

REPRONOVA provides legal and strategic advice in assisted reproduction. We agree whose interests we represent, what work we undertake and where local professional involvement is required. An adviser’s authority to communicate with an agency, clinic or other participant is defined by the client’s mandate.

Independent judgement

Our advice is directed by the agreed task and the client’s interests within the law. We do not sell clinic packages or recruit or match surrogates and donors. We do not accept hidden referral, success or commission fees from clinics, agencies or programme operators. Any relevant commercial relationship is disclosed before an introduction or recommendation.

Conflicts of interest

Before accepting work, we consider whether existing relationships or instructions could impair independent advice. Acting for one participant in a reproductive programme does not make us the adviser to every participant. If a conflict prevents us from acting, we explain the limitation and, where appropriate, suggest that separate advice be obtained.

Consent and confidentiality

We respect each participant’s ability to make informed decisions about matters affecting them. Our work should not be used to pressure a surrogate, donor or intended parent into signing documents or accepting medical decisions. Information is shared only for an identified lawful purpose and with appropriate regard for confidentiality. We do not promise legal privilege where it does not arise under the applicable law.

Evidence and professional limits

We distinguish verified facts from assumptions and identify gaps that could affect the advice. We explain the scope and limits of reviews, legal opinions and introductions. Where a task requires specialist medical knowledge or local legal authority, we identify that need. We do not promise a particular medical, administrative or judicial outcome.

Fees and accountability

We agree the work and fees in advance and explain how additional tasks will be authorised. Clients may raise a concern at info@repronovalegal.com. We review concerns in light of the agreed scope, records and applicable obligations.
