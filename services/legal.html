Independent legal audit and second opinion

An independent review is useful when you have received a programme proposal, a set of agreements or advice whose assumptions you want to examine. We assess the legal structure and supporting evidence within a defined scope, identifying inconsistencies and matters that require further enquiry.

Providers and contractual relationships

The organisation promoting a programme, the party signing the agreement, the clinic providing treatment and the payment recipient may have different roles. We examine how those roles are documented and, where included in the assignment, check available corporate and licensing information. The availability and reliability of public records differ between jurisdictions.

Payments and programme documents

We review the relationship between the quoted price, payment schedule and contractual services. Questions may concern additional charges, release conditions, refunds or the terms of a proposed escrow arrangement. We also look for missing schedules, conflicting provisions and promises that do not appear in the signed documents.

A second legal opinion

If you already have legal advice, we can examine its reasoning, assumptions and supporting documents. We identify areas of agreement, questions left unanswered and any further information needed. A second opinion may confirm an earlier assessment as well as identify points requiring reconsideration.

Findings and next steps

The deliverable sets out what was examined, the findings, the limits of the review and the issues to resolve. This is a legal assessment; it does not establish treatment success rates, certify a clinic’s clinical quality or guarantee a provider’s solvency.

Send a short description of the proposed arrangement and the decision you need to make. We will agree the review questions and document requirements before starting.
