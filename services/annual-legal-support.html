Annual Legal Support for Clinics, Agencies and Gamete Banks

Organisations working in assisted reproduction regularly need legal input on agreements, patient communications and documentation. Our annual service provides comprehensive prepaid support for a 12-month term under a defined list of services and an agreed volume of work.

An annual scope tailored to your organisation

Before the engagement begins, we review the organisation’s activities and recurring needs. We agree which tasks are included, the applicable jurisdictions, the responsible contacts, response arrangements and the limits of the service. The annual fee is agreed and paid in advance under the contract.

What support can include

The agreed programme may cover ongoing consultations, drafting and reviewing contracts, maintaining template documents, consent and donor documentation, data protection questions, and legal review of correspondence with patients or professional partners. Planned staff briefings or reviews of a particular process can be included expressly.

Managing work during the year

Requests are submitted through the agreed channel and prioritised with the organisation. Reporting and review arrangements allow both sides to track the work undertaken and identify issues requiring a separate decision. We do not describe the service as unlimited: included tasks, capacity and any exclusions are recorded in the engagement.

Work outside the annual scope

Additional projects, litigation, local counsel and other external professional costs require separate agreement unless expressly included. The contract also sets out the arrangements for the end of the term, any renewal, termination and treatment of prepaid fees.

To discuss annual support, tell us what your organisation does, where it operates, the main categories of documents it uses and the legal work it expects during the year.
