Gamete and embryo donation legal advisory

Donation arrangements can connect donors, recipients, banks and clinics operating under different rules. We review the legal documentation relevant to the proposed donation and use of eggs, sperm or embryos, helping clients understand what has been authorised and what still needs confirmation.

Consent and permitted use

The review can address the scope of consent, restrictions recorded by the provider, future use, withdrawal questions and the relationship between consent forms and contractual documents. We consider whose consent is required and what evidence the receiving organisation expects to hold.

Donor information and legal status

We examine the terms relating to anonymity or identity release, access to donor information, compensation or expenses, and the legal implications of donor involvement. A donor profile or a contractual description does not by itself answer every question about information rights or legal parentage. The relevant rules and documents need to be assessed together.

Arrangements involving more than one country

Where material or documentation crosses borders, we identify the requirements to clarify with the sending and receiving organisations. Questions can concern consent evidence, traceability, disclosure and the records needed for treatment or later enquiries.

What we provide

An agreed assignment may produce a written assessment, comments on donor or recipient documents, proposed amendments and questions for the bank, clinic or local adviser. We can advise individuals or organisations, subject to the interests involved and conflict checks.

For an initial enquiry, describe the proposed arrangement and countries. Please do not send identifiable donor records through the general contact form; we will explain what material is required and how to provide it.
