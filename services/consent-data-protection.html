Consent, donor information and reproductive data protection

Reproductive services generate information that may remain important long after treatment. We advise on the legal documentation and processes used to obtain consent, maintain donor records and handle access, disclosure and confidentiality questions.

Reviewing the documentation and process

For organisations, an assignment can examine how information is collected, which notices and consent forms are used, who records changes and how responsibilities are allocated. We look for gaps between the documents and the actual sequence of contact, treatment, storage and record retention.

Access to donor and treatment information

Individuals and organisations may need advice on a request for records, donor information or an explanation of a disclosure restriction. We assess the request against the relevant circumstances, rules and records. The interests of a recipient, donor and donor-conceived person may differ and need separate consideration.

Sharing, retention and continuity

Work can address arrangements with service providers, cross-border disclosure, record retention and the continued availability of information when a clinic or bank changes ownership or closes. The purpose is to identify the applicable legal questions and document the responsibilities needed in the actual operating model.

Scope and deliverables

The result may include a findings report, revised notices or forms, a response to a defined information request, or recommendations for a particular process. Technical security testing and formal certification require their own specialist work and are not implied by a legal review.

For the first enquiry, describe the organisation or request and the issue to resolve. Use anonymised examples until the appropriate document-sharing arrangements have been established.
