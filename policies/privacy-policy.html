Privacy Policy

Version dated 13 September 2026. Effective upon publication.

1 Who is responsible for your information

REPRONOVA Fertility Legal Solutions is a project operated by VERDYS CONSULTING s.r.o., Company ID (IČO) 02830841, registered office at Navrátilova 1334/16, Nové Město, 110 00 Praha 1, Czech Republic, registered in the Commercial Register maintained by the Municipal Court in Prague, Section C, File 224160. We are the controller of personal data processed for the website, initial enquiries, professional cooperation requests and our newsletter. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that company. For privacy enquiries or to exercise your rights, contact info@repronovalegal.com or write to our registered office. Telephone: +420 737 790 512.

2 Scope of this policy

This policy explains how we handle information when you visit repronovalegal.com, contact us or subscribe to updates. If you engage us for a specific matter, we will provide any additional information needed for the processing involved in that engagement. Independent clinics, agencies, lawyers and other providers have their own responsibilities for information they receive.

3 Information you provide

An initial enquiry may include your name, email address, country of residence, optional telephone number, the countries involved, your message and relevant deadlines. Professional cooperation requests may also include your organisation, role, services and business contact details. Newsletter records include your email address and records of your subscription choices and confirmation. Fields marked as required enable us to handle the request. Providing an optional telephone number is not a condition of receiving a reply. If essential contact information is missing, we may be unable to respond.

4 Technical information and sources

Website infrastructure may process IP addresses, request times, browser and device information, requested pages and security events to deliver and protect the site. Our Cookie Policy explains storage on your device and external resource requests. We normally obtain enquiry information directly from you. A representative may contact us on your behalf, and relevant business information may come from public registers or professional websites. Where we obtain personal data indirectly, we provide the applicable privacy information within the required period unless a lawful exception applies. We do not treat publicly available contact information as permission to send marketing.

5 Sensitive information

Please do not include medical records, genetic data, identity documents or identifying details about children, donors or surrogates in the initial website form. If your matter requires sensitive information, we first agree what is needed and how it should be shared. Processing health, genetic or other special category data requires both an Article 6 GDPR basis and an applicable Article 9 condition. Depending on the matter, this may include explicit consent or necessity for establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. We identify the applicable condition before undertaking that processing. Submitting an enquiry does not itself provide explicit consent for every subsequent use of sensitive data.

6 Purposes and lawful bases

We use enquiry information to understand your request, communicate with you and consider the proposed work. Where you request steps towards a contract to which you would be a party, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. General enquiries and communications with representatives of organisations are handled on the basis of our legitimate interests in responding to correspondence and managing professional relationships under Article 6(1)(f). We may process the minimum necessary information to identify conflicts, prevent misuse, protect the website and establish or defend legal claims. The basis is our legitimate interests in those activities, balanced against the rights of the individuals concerned. Where a specific legal obligation requires processing, we rely on Article 6(1)(c). Our newsletter is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a). Optional cookies or similar technologies are used only with the consent required for that purpose. Neither a contact request nor a cooperation enquiry automatically subscribes you to marketing.

7 Newsletter choices

You can subscribe to REPRONOVA legal updates, articles, event announcements and information about our services. We ask you to confirm your email address before activating the subscription. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in an email or by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing lawfully undertaken before withdrawal. We retain a limited record of your opt-out where necessary to honour it. Transactional replies about your own enquiry are separate from newsletter communications. We do not use individual email open tracking or personalised marketing profiles.

8 Recipients and service providers

Access is limited to people who need the information for the relevant task. This may include authorised staff and advisers, hosting and website support providers, email and form processing providers, newsletter providers, and professional advisers assisting with administration or legal claims. Processors act under appropriate contractual instructions; independent providers remain responsible for their own processing. We may disclose information to a court, regulator or other authority where required or otherwise lawfully necessary. An initial enquiry is not automatically circulated to agencies or clinics. Where a specialist introduction or local advice is needed, we clarify the proposed recipient and the information to be shared. These categories cover the providers used to operate our website and communications. You may contact us for information about recipients of your personal data. External content delivery services used by the website are described in our Cookie Policy. We do not sell personal data or provide enquiry details to third parties for their own marketing.

9 International transfers

Where a recipient outside the European Economic Area can access personal data, we assess the transfer separately from the lawful basis for the underlying processing. We use an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses with any necessary supplementary measures. A statutory derogation is used only where its particular requirements are met. International access may arise through website or communication infrastructure, or where your matter requires a professional in another country. Before sharing case information internationally, we explain the relevant recipient, purpose and applicable transfer arrangement. You may request details of destinations, recipients and safeguards, including how to obtain a copy, by contacting us. Protection of confidential information may require limited redactions.

10 Retention

We retain personal data only for the relevant purpose and apply the following retention rules. Initial enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted or anonymised within six months after the last substantive communication. Routine security and access logs under our control are retained for up to 30 days. Unconfirmed newsletter requests are deleted after seven days. Active subscription details are kept until you unsubscribe or we discontinue the newsletter. Limited consent evidence is retained for three years after withdrawal. Minimal suppression records are kept while the relevant mailing list operates, solely to prevent further marketing. Engagement files are retained according to the service, record-keeping duties and relevant limitation periods. Records for a specific complaint, incident, investigation or claim may be kept longer where necessary or legally required. Restricted backup copies are replaced within 90 days. Restoration must preserve deletion requests and unsubscribe choices.

11 Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may object to direct marketing at any time, without giving a reason. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it. Contact info@repronovalegal.com to make a request. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension is needed because of complexity or the number of requests, we explain this within that period. Requests are normally free of charge, subject to the limited exceptions in the GDPR. Certain records may need to be retained despite a deletion request, for example to comply with a legal duty or defend a claim.

12 Complaints

You may complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, Czech Republic. Its website is https://uoou.gov.cz/. You may also contact the competent supervisory authority in the country of your habitual residence or workplace, or where you believe an infringement occurred. You do not have to contact us first.

13 Security and automated decisions

We apply access controls and technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information and the risks involved. Internet communications cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use the document-sharing method agreed with us for sensitive material. Decisions about accepting an enquiry or engagement are made by people. We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you solely by automated processing. If a new processing activity requires additional information, we will provide it before that activity begins.

14 Changes and related information

We update this policy when our processing arrangements or relevant requirements change. The effective date identifies the published version. A new version does not itself provide consent for a new use of data. For information about storage on your device, please read our Cookie Policy.
