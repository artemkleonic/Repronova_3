About REPRONOVA

REPRONOVA Fertility Legal Solutions offers a new format of legal and consulting services in human assisted reproduction. Clients can engage us for a defined question, a written assessment or continuing assistance as a programme or professional matter develops.

Experience applied to the particular task

Our work brings together legal analysis, an understanding of reproductive medicine and experience of resolving complex matters involving different jurisdictions. Led by Sergii Antonov, REPRONOVA draws on the experience of its experts and collaborators to identify the people, documents and legal questions that matter to an assignment. Where a task requires local legal advice or specialist input, we clarify that role and coordinate the relevant work.

A connected range of services

The breadth of our services allows a client to move from an initial assessment to practical implementation. An enquiry may begin with the review of a programme proposal and continue with contract amendments, correspondence with providers, planning for parentage or the resolution of a difficulty. Each stage has an agreed scope and deliverable.

For intended parents, this includes independent legal advisory and representation during a surrogacy programme. Within agreed authority, we communicate with agencies and clinics, request documents and explanations, follow up on contractual commitments and help address unresolved issues. Clients remain involved in decisions affecting their programme.

Surrogates and donors can seek independent advice about documents, consent, information rights, payments and compensation questions. We identify whose interests we represent and assess conflicts before accepting a matter.

Support for organisations

Clinics, agencies, gamete and embryo banks and other professional providers can engage us to develop or review contracts, consent forms, internal procedures and documentation for international activities. We also assist with legal opinions, disputes, research and professional training.

For organisations that need continuing support, we offer comprehensive prepaid legal assistance for twelve months. The engagement sets out the agreed list and volume of services, reporting arrangements and the treatment of work outside that scope.

Independence and working arrangements

REPRONOVA provides legal and strategic advice. We do not recruit or match surrogates or donors, and we do not sell clinic packages. We do not accept hidden referral, success or commission fees. Any relevant third-party payment arrangement must be disclosed and must not compromise our advice.

Before work begins, we clarify the assignment, the information needed, the proposed output and fees. A consultation, a written opinion and ongoing representation involve different responsibilities; the engagement records which of these the client is commissioning.

REPRONOVA Fertility Legal Solutions is operated by VERDYS Consulting s.r.o., Czech Republic. Consultations and professional cooperation can be arranged remotely.
