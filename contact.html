What to include

Tell us the countries involved, the type of help you need and any relevant deadline. At this stage, a short description is sufficient. If documents are needed, we will agree an appropriate way to share them.

How your enquiry progresses

We review the request, consider whether we can accept the matter and clarify the proposed work. The consultation format, scope and fees are agreed before work begins.
