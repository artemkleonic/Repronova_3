Contract review before signing

A programme may involve several agreements, schedules and consent documents. Before signing, parents need to understand which organisation promises each service and what happens if the proposed arrangements change. We review the documents as a connected set within the agreed scope.

The obligations you are accepting

We examine services, payment stages, additional costs, client obligations and the provisions governing delay, changes, termination and refunds. The review also addresses how notices are given, which documents form part of the contract and how disagreements are to be handled.

Turning concerns into clear questions

You receive comments explaining the issues that matter and, where included, proposed wording or amendments. We identify promises made in correspondence or a proposal that should be clarified in the agreement. Negotiations with the provider can be included separately or undertaken through our representation service.

If you have already signed

A review can still help you understand your position. We assess the signed terms, the circumstances and the question you now face. We do not assume that an unfavourable provision can simply be removed or that the provider must accept an amendment.

Tell us which agreement you want reviewed, the countries involved and any signing or payment deadline. Keep the complete set of schedules and related correspondence available. Sensitive documents should be provided only through the channel agreed for the review.
