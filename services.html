Legal and Consulting Services

Explore legal and consulting support for individuals and organisations involved in assisted reproduction. Services range from a consultation or document review to independent representation during a programme and annual legal support for clinics, agencies and gamete banks. Open a service below to see what it covers and how to enquire.

Independent Legal Advisory and Representation for Intended Parents

An independent legal adviser on your side throughout your surrogacy programme. We handle correspondence with your agency, clinic and other providers, monitor contractual commitments, and help resolve issues on your behalf within an agreed scope of authority.

LEARN MORE

Cross-border surrogacy legal advisory

Legal risk assessment, jurisdictional strategy, contract review, parentage and citizenship planning for cross-border surrogacy matters.

LEARN MORE

Independent legal audit and second opinion

Review of clinics, agencies, contracts, payment structures, documentation and legal risks before clients commit to a programme or transfer funds.

LEARN MORE

Annual Legal Support for Clinics, Agencies and Gamete Banks

Comprehensive prepaid legal support for a 12-month term, tailored to an agreed scope of services. Ongoing consultations, contract work and documentation support for clinics, agencies and gamete banks.

LEARN MORE

Gamete and embryo donation legal advisory

Legal support for consent, anonymity, compensation, parentage exclusion, data protection and cross-border use of gametes and embryos.

LEARN MORE

Fertility law consultations and legal opinions

Individual consultations and written legal opinions on defined questions in assisted reproduction. Understand the legal issues, the supporting reasoning and the next steps relevant to your circumstances.

LEARN MORE

Fertility contract review and drafting

Review and preparation of fertility-related agreements, amendments and supporting documents. Clarify responsibilities, payments, consent-related provisions and the consequences of changes or termination.

LEARN MORE

Parentage, citizenship and travel documentation

Legal planning for parentage, birth registration, nationality and a child’s travel documents after assisted reproduction. Identify the relevant procedures, evidence and professional responsibilities before they become urgent.

LEARN MORE

Fertility preservation and deferred parenthood

Legal advice on freezing and storing reproductive material for future use. Review consent, storage agreements, renewal arrangements and the implications of changes in personal circumstances or provider.

LEARN MORE

Posthumous reproduction and future-use decisions

Legal analysis of consent and the proposed use of reproductive material following death or loss of decision-making capacity. Clarify the documents, applicable questions and steps requiring specialist involvement.

LEARN MORE

Gamete and embryo storage and cross-border transfer

Legal review of storage, release and international transfer arrangements for eggs, sperm and embryos. Address consent, provider responsibilities, transfer documents and the continuity of associated records.

LEARN MORE

Consent, donor information and reproductive data protection

Review of consent processes, donor records and the handling of reproductive information. Clarify documentation, access requests, confidentiality, retention and responsibilities when information is shared or transferred.

LEARN MORE

Legal support for clinics, banks and agencies

Project-based legal support for fertility clinics, gamete banks, surrogacy agencies and medical tourism agencies. Develop contracts, document responsibilities and address specific operational or client-related legal questions.

LEARN MORE

Programme interruptions and provider closure

Legal support when a fertility programme is interrupted or a provider closes. Review contractual options, payments, access to reproductive material and records, and the arrangements needed to continue elsewhere.

LEARN MORE

Reproductive law research and professional training

Commissioned legal research, comparative analysis and professional training on assisted reproduction. Focused materials and sessions for organisations, advisers and teams working with reproductive programmes and documentation.

LEARN MORE
