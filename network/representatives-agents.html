Local Representatives and Coordination Professionals

Clients and professional organisations may need someone locally to coordinate appointments, communicate with service providers or assist with administrative arrangements. REPRONOVA can help identify candidates for a defined task and clarify how their work will be supervised and reported.

How we can assist

We can help define the assignment, request information about experience and prepare or review the terms of engagement. The scope might include arranging document collection where permitted, following up on administrative requests, organising meetings or coordinating communications in the local language.

Defining the assignment

The engagement should specify what the representative may do, what requires prior approval and whether any formal authority is needed. Legal advice, court representation and medical decisions require the relevant professional competence and authority. A local coordination role does not by itself authorise a person to act for REPRONOVA.

Cooperate with REPRONOVA

We invite experienced local coordinators and representatives to describe their location, languages and services. Cooperation is subject to an agreed mandate, confidentiality arrangements and transparent remuneration. Appointment does not automatically confer exclusivity or authority to bind REPRONOVA or its clients.

Tell us what you need

Please describe the location, task, expected duration and whether attendance in person is required. For legal representation of intended parents, the scope will be discussed as a separate professional engagement.
