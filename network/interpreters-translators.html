Interpreters and Translators

REPRONOVA can help identify interpreters and translators for fertility-related legal and administrative matters. We begin with the language pair, type of assignment and intended use, so that the professional’s experience and any formal requirements can be considered.

How we can assist

Assignments may include translation of agreements, consent forms, civil status documents or legal opinions, and interpretation during consultations or meetings. We can help define the brief, coordinate terminology questions and agree how confidential documents will be exchanged.

Defining the assignment

Some recipients require a particular form of certified or sworn translation. Those requirements should be established for the destination and purpose before the work is commissioned. The engagement should also clarify deadlines, revision arrangements and whether attendance in person or remote interpretation is needed.

Cooperate with REPRONOVA

We welcome enquiries from translators and interpreters with experience in medical, reproductive or family law terminology. Please state your working languages, location, relevant qualifications, certification where applicable and the assignments you are available to undertake.

Tell us what you need

Indicate the languages, document type or meeting format, destination authority if applicable and deadline. For translation enquiries, an approximate word or page count is sufficient at the initial stage.
