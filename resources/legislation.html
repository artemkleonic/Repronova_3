Legislation and Regulatory Sources

Selected sources for researching assisted reproduction, parentage and related documentation. This collection is an orientation tool, not a complete statement of the law in any country.

Read the source in context

Check the jurisdiction, the version in force at the relevant time and any related regulations or procedures. Proposed legislation, professional guidance and enacted law have different status. A translated text may not reflect the latest amendments.

Ukraine Family Code | National legislation

A starting point for questions concerning family status and parentage. Article 123 addresses the determination of a child’s origin in circumstances involving assisted reproduction. Consult the official consolidated text and relevant procedural rules.

Ukraine Ministry of Health Order No 787 of 9 September 2013 | National regulation

The procedure governing the use of assisted reproductive technologies in Ukraine. Read the relevant sections together with the applicable family, healthcare and registration provisions.

Georgia Law on Health Care | National legislation

Includes provisions on assisted reproduction, including Articles 141, 143 and 144. Select the latest consolidated publication on Matsne. An English translation may lag behind changes to the Georgian text.

Armenia Law on Human Reproductive Health and Reproductive Rights | National legislation

The official ARLIS text provides the statutory starting point for reproductive rights and assisted reproduction in Armenia. Review the provisions relevant to eligibility, participants and the proposed programme.

United States California Family Code Section 7962 | State legislation

A California provision concerning assisted reproduction agreements for gestational carriers. It addresses agreement requirements and separate legal representation. This is state legislation and should not be applied as a nationwide US rule.

European Union General Data Protection Regulation | EU regulation

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 is relevant to the handling of personal data where its scope applies. Articles 5, 6, 9 and 13 concern processing principles, lawful bases, special categories of data and information supplied when data is collected.

European Union Substances of Human Origin Regulation | EU regulation with future application dates

Regulation (EU) 2024/1938 concerns quality and safety standards for substances of human origin. Most provisions apply from 7 August 2027. Use Article 87 to distinguish entry into force from the dates on which particular provisions apply.

Regulatory guidance and international work

The HFEA publishes guidance for the UK fertility sector, including consent to treatment and storage. The HCCH Parentage / Surrogacy Project provides international research and project documents; it is not itself an applicable convention on recognition of parentage.

Need an assessment for a particular country

Read our jurisdictional guides or ask us to examine the legal sources relevant to your proposed arrangement. The location of treatment may be only one of the jurisdictions that matters.
