Organising a Response to a Programme Dispute

When a problem arises, an organised record and a clear request can help your adviser assess the available options.

1 Address immediate needs

If the situation involves an immediate health concern, seek appropriate medical assistance. Tell the legal adviser about any court, insurance, payment or other deadline. Do not assume an informal discussion suspends a deadline.

2 Record the facts

Create a chronology of the problem and distinguish confirmed events from assumptions. Preserve original agreements, messages, invoices and records already lawfully held. Do not edit originals or publish other participants’ private information.

3 Define the response you need

Identify whether the immediate objective is an explanation, access to records, continued performance, a corrective step, a refund or compensation. Separate urgent requests from longer-term claims.

4 Review responsibility and evidence

Ask the adviser to identify the relevant contractual parties, notice requirements and possible legal or insurance routes. Cases involving treatment quality, loss of material or injury may require independent technical or medical evidence.

5 Agree the communication strategy

Decide who will communicate with each provider and which proposals need your approval. Consider whether negotiation or mediation is suitable, alongside any formal action needed to protect the position. Review settlement terms before accepting payment or signing a release.

Using this guide

These materials provide general information and questions for discussion. An individual legal assessment depends on the facts, documents and jurisdictions involved.
