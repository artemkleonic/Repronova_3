Questions to Ask About Programme Payments and Escrow

The word escrow does not explain who controls funds or when money can leave the account. Review the actual documents and payment process.

1 Establish who holds the money

Identify the account holder, bank and proposed administrator. Ask what legal structure governs the funds, whether they are segregated and whose rights apply if the administrator or another participant becomes insolvent.

2 Examine the release conditions

Identify who can request, approve and execute a payment. Ask which documents are required and whether you receive notice before release. Clarify whether the administrator checks performance or only specified paperwork.

3 Model a disputed payment

Ask what happens if you contest an invoice, a milestone is disputed or a provider stops responding. Identify the process for holding a disputed amount and resolving the disagreement, including any administrator discretion.

4 Account for the full programme

Review deposits, top-ups, administrator fees, currency conversion, unused balances and refunds. Identify how emergency or additional costs would be approved. Compare these terms with the programme agreements and insurance arrangements.

5 Request a written assessment

Ask the adviser to explain the protections supported by the documents and any unresolved questions. Do not assume a particular account structure removes programme risk. Changes to payment instructions should be independently confirmed using previously established contact details.

Using this guide

These materials provide general information and questions for discussion. An individual legal assessment depends on the facts, documents and jurisdictions involved.
