WHO publishes its first global infertility guideline

Policy update · Guideline announced 28 November 2025

Policy update

Guideline announced 28 November 2025

On 28 November 2025, the World Health Organization announced its first global guideline on infertility. It contains 40 recommendations intended to support safer, more accessible and affordable care and to assist countries in strengthening services. WHO also identified fertility preservation and third-party reproduction among subjects for future guideline editions.

What the publication means

The guideline is a reference for health policy and clinical practice. Its publication should not be described as creating a single international legal framework for surrogacy or donation. Questions about who may access treatment, the validity of consent and legal parentage still require assessment under the relevant national rules.

A useful review for service providers

Clinics can use the publication as a reason to review how they explain their services, document patient decisions and communicate costs and limitations. Legal and clinical teams should identify which changes concern professional recommendations and which arise from binding requirements. Any update to patient documents needs to reflect the services the clinic actually provides.

How REPRONOVA can assist

We support clinics and professional organisations with consent and contract reviews, legal research and revisions to patient-facing documents. This work can be commissioned as a defined project or included in an agreed annual legal support package. Clinical interpretation remains with the appropriately qualified medical professionals.
