Armenia and the need for an individual eligibility assessment

Jurisdictional analysis · Consolidated legislation reviewed 13 September 2026

Jurisdictional analysis

Consolidated legislation reviewed 13 September 2026

Article 12 of Armenia’s law on reproductive health and reproductive rights contains specific restrictions on access to surrogacy. In particular, it excludes foreign nationals who are not of Armenian origin from using an Armenian-citizen surrogate. The provision needs to be read alongside the other eligibility conditions and the documents relevant to the intended parents’ circumstances.

Avoid a general assumption of availability

A programme advertised in Armenia should be assessed for the particular participants and proposed arrangement. The review should distinguish citizenship from Armenian origin and identify what evidence is relied upon. Changing one participant or moving an element of the programme requires a fresh assessment rather than an assumption that the original advice still applies.

Look beyond entry into the programme

For an international family, the review should also address the intended use of donor material, the planned birth location and the documents required afterwards. The parents need a coherent account of the route through the programme and the legal steps connected with the child’s status abroad.

How REPRONOVA can assist

We can review the proposed structure, identify eligibility questions and coordinate Armenian and home-country advice. Where material has already been created or funds committed, the work can focus on the available documents, unresolved obligations and the options that can be assessed on the facts.
