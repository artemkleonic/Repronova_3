Surrogates & Donors

Independent legal support focused on informed consent, rights, privacy, compensation terms and cross-border use of reproductive material.

Independent legal advice

Clear explanation of rights, obligations and legal consequences before signing surrogacy, donor or ART-related documentation.

LEARN MORE

Consent and data protection

Legal review of consent forms, anonymity terms, data processing, medical documentation and future use of gametes or embryos.

LEARN MORE

Compensation following complications or death

Legal assistance with compensation claims following serious complications, injury or the death of a surrogate or donor. We assess contractual provisions, insurance benefits and potential claims, including enquiries from bereaved families or authorised representatives.

LEARN MORE

Compensation, expenses and payment terms

Understand the agreed terms for compensation, expenses and reimbursement. We review payment conditions, deductions, supporting documents and what happens if participation changes or ends.

LEARN MORE

Representation in programme communications

Independent legal support in correspondence and discussions with clinics, agencies and other participants. We request explanations, address contractual concerns and communicate on your behalf within agreed authority.

LEARN MORE

Changes to participation and consent

Legal advice when proposed terms, procedures or personal circumstances change. Understand how the documents address amended consent, withdrawal questions, termination and the consequences of a proposed decision.

LEARN MORE

Treatment costs and insurance arrangements

Legal review of documents addressing treatment costs, insurance and support if complications occur. Clarify the responsible parties, stated coverage, exclusions and the process for requesting payment or reimbursement.

LEARN MORE

Disputes, complaints and mediation

Support with disagreements about payments, documents, privacy or programme obligations. We assess the issue, help prepare a reasoned response and discuss negotiation or mediation where appropriate.

LEARN MORE

Cross-border donation and future use

Advice for donors on the proposed use of eggs, sperm or embryos in another country. Review consent, recorded restrictions, information-sharing and the documents governing storage, transfer and future use.

LEARN MORE

No recruitment or matching

REPRONOVA does not recruit, match or place surrogates or donors. Our role is independent legal and strategic advisory.
