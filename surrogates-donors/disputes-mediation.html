Disputes, complaints and mediation

When a disagreement arises, it is useful to establish the facts, identify the responsible party and decide what outcome you are seeking. We assist surrogates and donors with defined legal issues involving agencies, clinics and other participants, subject to conflict checks.

Reviewing the problem

We consider the agreements, relevant records and correspondence. Questions may concern unpaid amounts, expenses, confidentiality, access to documents, changes to participation or the conduct of the programme. Where the issue involves medical care, an appropriate clinical or expert assessment may also be needed.

A response supported by the documents

The agreed work can include a written request, complaint or assessment of possible contractual or compensation claims. We identify the evidence supporting your position and the questions that remain unresolved. The existence of a disagreement does not by itself establish liability or a right to compensation.

Negotiation and mediation

We may assist in negotiating a proposed resolution when instructed to advise or represent you. Neutral mediation or facilitation is a different role and requires separate agreement and consideration of conflicts. The role of the professional and the treatment of confidential information must be understood by the participants.

If proceedings or locally regulated work are needed, the relevant professional arrangements are discussed separately. In your initial message, provide a short chronology, the response received and the outcome you want to achieve. Please identify any stated deadline.
