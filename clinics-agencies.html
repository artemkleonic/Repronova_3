Clinics Agencies and Banks

REPRONOVA provides legal and consulting support for fertility clinics, surrogacy and fertility agencies, gamete and embryo banks and international medical tourism organisations. An engagement can address a particular document or problem, a defined project or continuing legal support throughout the year.

Documents that support your activities

We develop and review client agreements, donor and surrogacy agreements, informed consent documentation, arrangements with professional providers and internal procedures. The work considers how documents are used in practice and whether responsibilities, payments, information flows and decision-making are consistently recorded.

International work and complex questions

For organisations working across borders, we can review the proposed legal structure, prepare written opinions and coordinate questions requiring local professional advice. Assignments may concern donor information, storage and transfer of reproductive material, programme interruption, parentage-related documentation or the handling of a sensitive individual matter.

Annual prepaid legal support

We offer comprehensive prepaid legal assistance for twelve months under an agreed list and volume of services. The annual engagement can combine consultations, document work, legal correspondence and specified support with ongoing matters. The agreement records the scope, communication and reporting arrangements, fees and the treatment of additional work. It does not imply unlimited services or automatic coverage of litigation and external professional costs.

Difficulties complaints and disputes

We can help examine the facts and documents, prepare responses and discuss possible solutions to disagreements with clients or other organisations. If a neutral mediation or facilitation process is appropriate, that role must be separately agreed. We assess conflicts before taking instructions and do not represent opposing interests in the same dispute.

Research training and cooperation

Professional clients can also commission legal research, staff training and practical materials addressing recurring issues in reproductive medicine. If your organisation wishes to explore cooperation, tell us what you provide, where you operate and which type of support you need.
